## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

[err-00021] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Avoid conflating 'reserved for issuance' with 'authorized under the plan'. The former relates to common stock reserved for future issuance (CommonStockCapitalSharesReservedForFutureIssuance), while the latter relates to the number of shares authorized under a share-based compensation plan (ShareBasedCompensationArrangementByShareBasedPaymentAwardNumberOfSharesAuthorized). Always check the exact wording: 'reserved for issuance' signals capital shares reserved, even if the shares are reserved for equity awards.
## PROBLEM-SOLVING HEURISTICS

[ph-00008] helpful=2 harmful=0 :: Before applying debt-specific cues, first identify the domain of the sentence (debt vs. equity/share-based compensation). For equity plans, focus on plan-related phrases (authorized, available for grant, granted, RSUs) rather than debt cues like 'million of' or 'percent'.
## CONTEXT CLUES & INDICATORS

[ctx-00001] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: For debt instruments, a numeric value immediately followed by 'million of' (e.g., '$750 million of') indicates the principal/face amount, which maps to DebtInstrumentFaceAmount.
[ctx-00002] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: A numeric value followed by 'percent' (e.g., '3.25-percent') indicates a stated interest rate, which maps to DebtInstrumentInterestRateStatedPercentage.
[ctx-00004] helpful=1 harmful=0 :: A numeric value preceded by 'weighted average interest rate' (e.g., 'weighted average interest rate ... was 5.86%') indicates a weighted average interest rate, which maps to DebtWeightedAverageInterestRate (not DebtInstrumentInterestRateStatedPercentage).
[ctx-00005] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: A numeric value preceded by 'borrowing capacity' (e.g., 'maximum borrowing capacity of $50.0 million') indicates the maximum borrowing capacity of a line of credit, which maps to LineOfCreditFacilityMaximumBorrowingCapacity (not DebtInstrumentFaceAmount).
[ctx-00006] helpful=0 harmful=1 :: For share-based compensation plans, a numeric value followed by 'shares ... may be issued pursuant to awards granted under the [year] Plan' indicates the total shares authorized under the plan, mapping to ShareBasedCompensationArrangementByShareBasedPaymentAwardNumberOfSharesAuthorized (not NumberOfSharesAvailableForGrant).
[ctx-00007] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Distinguish 'authorized' from 'available for grant': phrases like 'may be issued pursuant to awards' or 'aggregate of X shares ... under the Plan' signal authorized shares, while 'available for grant' or 'remaining available' explicitly indicate shares not yet granted.
[ctx-00009] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: When a numeric share count is described as issued to fund or partially fund an acquisition (e.g., 'partially funded the purchase price ... by issuing a total of X shares/units'), tag it as BusinessAcquisitionEquityInterestsIssuedOrIssuableNumberOfSharesIssued, not SaleOfStockNumberOfSharesIssuedInTransaction. The acquisition-consideration context overrides generic stock-sale tags.
[ctx-00010] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: When a percentage describes the parent/company's total ownership interest in a joint venture or subsidiary (e.g., 'increasing the Company's total interest in the joint venture to X%'), tag it as MinorityInterestOwnershipPercentageByParent, not EquityMethodInvestmentOwnershipPercentage. Reserve EquityMethodInvestmentOwnershipPercentage for the investee-level equity method ownership percentage.
[ctx-00011] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: When a share count is described as sold or issued 'under this agreement' or 'during the period' (e.g., 'we sold X shares under this agreement'), tag it as StockIssuedDuringPeriodSharesNewIssues, not SaleOfStockNumberOfSharesIssuedInTransaction. Reserve SaleOfStockNumberOfSharesIssuedInTransaction for shares issued in a discrete sale transaction (e.g., a public offering or private placement).
[ctx-00012] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: When a sentence describes a company repurchasing shares (e.g., 'repurchased approximately X shares') and does not explicitly state that the shares are retired, tag the share count as TreasuryStockSharesAcquired rather than StockRepurchasedDuringPeriodShares. Reserve StockRepurchasedDuringPeriodShares for contexts where shares are repurchased and retired, or where the focus is on the repurchase activity itself rather than the resulting treasury stock.
[ctx-00013] helpful=1 harmful=0 :: When a sentence states that a company's charter or certificate of incorporation 'authorizes' a number of shares of a specific class (e.g., 'authorizes a total of X shares of common stock' or 'Y shares of preferred stock'), tag the share count as CommonStockSharesAuthorized or PreferredStockSharesAuthorized according to the stated class. Do not confuse this with share-based compensation plan authorized shares (ShareBasedCompensationArrangementByShareBasedPaymentAwardNumberOfSharesAuthorized).
[ctx-00014] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: When a percentage describes the portion of revenue derived from a particular source, channel, customer, or product (e.g., 'derived X% of revenue through our channel network'), tag it as ConcentrationRiskPercentage1. This applies to revenue concentration disclosures, not to ownership or interest percentages.
[ctx-00015] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: For acquisition consideration, if a cash amount is stated as part of the total consideration (e.g., 'consisting of $X million of cash') without any mention of cash acquired being netted, tag it as PaymentsToAcquireBusinessesGross. Reserve PaymentsToAcquireBusinessesNetOfCashAcquired for cases where the context explicitly indicates that cash acquired has been deducted from cash paid.
[ctx-00016] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: For share-based compensation vesting terms, a percentage describing how much of an award vests per period (e.g., 'vest 25% each year') maps to SharebasedCompensationArrangementBySharebasedPaymentAwardAwardVestingRightsPercentage, not to a period or rate tag.
[ctx-00017] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: For share-based compensation vesting terms, a duration phrase describing the vesting schedule (e.g., 'for four years' or 'over a four-year period') maps to ShareBasedCompensationArrangementByShareBasedPaymentAwardAwardVestingPeriod1. When a sentence contains both a vesting percentage and a vesting period (e.g., 'vest 25% each year for four years'), tag each entity separately with its respective tag.
[ctx-00018] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: A percentage described as a 'consolidated effective income tax rate' (e.g., '22.0%', '25.7%') maps to EffectiveIncomeTaxRateContinuingOperations, not to a generic tax rate or percentage tag.
[ctx-00019] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: For a loss contingency stated as a range (e.g., '$17,000 to $26,000'), both the lower and upper bound map to LossContingencyEstimateOfPossibleLoss. Do not tag the endpoints as separate minimum/maximum tags.
[ctx-00020] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: When a sentence states that shares are 'reserved for issuance' under an equity plan (e.g., 'reserved X shares of common stock for issuance under the Plan'), tag the share count as CommonStockCapitalSharesReservedForFutureIssuance, not as ShareBasedCompensationArrangementByShareBasedPaymentAwardNumberOfSharesAuthorized. The phrase 'reserved for issuance' indicates capital shares set aside, whereas 'authorized under the plan' refers to the plan's share authorization limit.
[ctx-00022] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: When a sentence describes a company repurchasing shares and states the 'aggregate purchase price' or 'cost' of the repurchased shares (e.g., 'for an aggregate purchase price of $X million'), tag the dollar amount as TreasuryStockValueAcquiredCostMethod, not as a generic repurchase amount.
[ctx-00023] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: When a sentence states that a portion of a stock repurchase program 'remained available for future purchases' (e.g., '$X million of the $Y million stock repurchase program ... remained available'), tag the remaining amount as StockRepurchaseProgramRemainingAuthorizedRepurchaseAmount1.
[ctx-00024] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: When a sentence refers to the total authorized amount of a stock repurchase program (e.g., 'the $Y million stock repurchase program approved on [date]'), tag that total amount as StockRepurchaseProgramAuthorizedAmount1, not as the remaining amount.
[ctx-00025] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: When a sentence states that a dividend is declared per share (e.g., 'declared a quarterly cash dividend of $X per share' or 'declared a dividend of $X on each share of common stock'), tag the per-share amount as CommonStockDividendsPerShareDeclared.
## OTHERS

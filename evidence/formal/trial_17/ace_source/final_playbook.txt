## STRATEGIES & INSIGHTS

[sai-00002] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: For debt-related disclosures, tag principal/face amounts of debt instruments with DebtInstrumentFaceAmount and stated interest rates with DebtInstrumentInterestRateStatedPercentage.
[sai-00003] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: When shares or units are issued as consideration to fund an acquisition purchase price, tag the number of shares/units with BusinessAcquisitionEquityInterestsIssuedOrIssuableNumberOfSharesIssued rather than generic stock issuance tags like SaleOfStockNumberOfSharesIssuedInTransaction.
[sai-00005] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: For stock repurchase disclosures, distinguish three related tags: use TreasuryStockValueAcquiredCostMethod for the aggregate cost/purchase price of shares repurchased; use StockRepurchaseProgramRemainingAuthorizedRepurchaseAmount1 for the amount remaining available under the program; and use StockRepurchaseProgramAuthorizedAmount1 for the total authorized program amount.
[sai-00006] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: For dividend disclosures, tag per-share dividend amounts (both regular quarterly and special dividends) with CommonStockDividendsPerShareDeclared.
[sai-00009] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: For effective income tax rate disclosures, tag the percentage with EffectiveIncomeTaxRateContinuingOperations, even when multiple rates are presented for different periods (e.g., quarterly and year-to-date).
[sai-00011] helpful=1 harmful=1 :: For stock-based compensation plan disclosures, map: total authorized shares under the plan to ShareBasedCompensationArrangementByShareBasedPaymentAwardNumberOfSharesAuthorized; shares available for future issuance to ShareBasedCompensationArrangementByShareBasedPaymentAwardNumberOfSharesAvailableForGrant; vesting percentage (e.g., '25% each year') to SharebasedCompensationArrangementBySharebasedPaymentAwardAwardVestingRightsPercentage; and vesting period (e.g., 'four years') to ShareBasedCompensationArrangementByShareBasedPaymentAwardAwardVestingPeriod1.
[sai-00013] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: For the number of shares issued in a new share issuance, use StockIssuedDuringPeriodSharesNewIssues, even when the transaction is described as 'sold' under an agreement. Avoid overly specific tags like SaleOfStockNumberOfSharesIssuedInTransaction unless the context clearly indicates a distinct sale transaction rather than a general issuance.
[sai-00015] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: For debt-related interest rate disclosures, tag the phrase 'weighted average interest rate' with DebtWeightedAverageInterestRate, not LineOfCreditFacilityInterestRateAtPeriodEnd. The latter is for a rate at a specific period end (e.g., a stated spread), whereas a weighted average over a period is a broader debt-level metric even when the underlying instrument is a line of credit.
[sai-00017] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: For stock-based compensation disclosures involving equity instruments other than options (e.g., RSUs), map the number of instruments granted to ShareBasedCompensationArrangementByShareBasedPaymentAwardEquityInstrumentsOtherThanOptionsGrantsInPeriod, and map the associated weighted average grant date fair value per instrument to ShareBasedCompensationArrangementByShareBasedPaymentAwardEquityInstrumentsOtherThanOptionsGrantsInPeriodWeightedAverageGrantDateFairValue.
[sai-00019] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: For business acquisition cash consideration, use PaymentsToAcquireBusinessesGross when the amount represents total cash paid (e.g., 'consisting of $X million of cash'), and use PaymentsToAcquireBusinessesNetOfCashAcquired only when the amount is explicitly net of cash acquired. The gross tag is appropriate for the cash portion of consideration stated without adjustment for cash acquired.
[sai-00021] helpful=1 harmful=0 :: Distinguish between 'authorized' shares under a specific plan and 'reserved for issuance' shares: use ShareBasedCompensationArrangementByShareBasedPaymentAwardNumberOfSharesAuthorized only when the sentence explicitly states the plan 'authorizes' a number of shares (e.g., 'the 2016 Plan authorizes X shares'); use CommonStockCapitalSharesReservedForFutureIssuance when the sentence says shares were 'reserved ... for issuance' (or 'reserved for future issuance') under stock plans, even if the context involves equity awards.
[sai-00023] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: For the number of shares repurchased under a stock repurchase program, prefer TreasuryStockSharesAcquired over StockRepurchasedDuringPeriodShares. TreasuryStockSharesAcquired is the more specific tag for shares acquired and held in treasury under a repurchase program, whereas StockRepurchasedDuringPeriodShares is a generic repurchase tag that may not capture treasury stock treatment.
[sai-00025] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: For share authorization disclosures in the certificate of incorporation, tag the number of common shares authorized with CommonStockSharesAuthorized and the number of preferred shares authorized with PreferredStockSharesAuthorized. These are distinct from stock-based compensation plan authorization tags.

## CONTEXT CLUES & INDICATORS

[ctx-00001] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: In debt disclosure sentences, a dollar amount followed by 'million' and describing notes/bonds (e.g., '$ 750 million of ... notes') indicates a principal/face amount, while a number followed by '- percent' or 'percent' indicates a stated interest rate.
[ctx-00004] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: A percentage describing the parent company's total ownership interest in a joint venture or subsidiary (e.g., 'increasing the Company's total interest in the joint venture to 99.6%') should be tagged with MinorityInterestOwnershipPercentageByParent, not EquityMethodInvestmentOwnershipPercentage.
[ctx-00007] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: In stock repurchase sentences, phrases like 'purchased ... shares ... for an aggregate purchase price of $X' indicate TreasuryStockValueAcquiredCostMethod; '$X ... remained available for future purchases' indicates StockRepurchaseProgramRemainingAuthorizedRepurchaseAmount1; and '$X ... stock repurchase program approved' indicates StockRepurchaseProgramAuthorizedAmount1.
[ctx-00008] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: A per-share dollar amount in a dividend declaration sentence (e.g., 'declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.08 ... on each share of common stock') indicates CommonStockDividendsPerShareDeclared.
[ctx-00010] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: In loss contingency sentences, phrases like 'estimates that the aggregate range of possible loss ... is $X to $Y' indicate LossContingencyEstimateOfPossibleLoss for both the low and high ends of the range; a separate reserved amount (e.g., 'has reserved $X') may use a different loss contingency tag.
[ctx-00012] helpful=1 harmful=1 :: In stock-based compensation sentences, phrases like 'total authorized of X million shares' indicate ShareBasedCompensationArrangementByShareBasedPaymentAwardNumberOfSharesAuthorized; 'X shares available for issuance' indicates ShareBasedCompensationArrangementByShareBasedPaymentAwardNumberOfSharesAvailableForGrant; 'vest X% each year' indicates SharebasedCompensationArrangementBySharebasedPaymentAwardAwardVestingRightsPercentage; and 'for X years' indicates ShareBasedCompensationArrangementByShareBasedPaymentAwardAwardVestingPeriod1.
[ctx-00014] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: When a sentence mentions both a number of shares and proceeds (e.g., 'Proceeds from the sale of the 107 shares were $2,632'), verify the entity being tagged: if the entity is the share count (e.g., '107'), tag it with a share-based tag such as StockIssuedDuringPeriodSharesNewIssues, not a proceeds tag like ProceedsFromIssuanceOfCommonStock.
[ctx-00016] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: When a sentence states 'current and maximum borrowing capacity of $X' for the same amount, tag that amount with LineOfCreditFacilityMaximumBorrowingCapacity rather than LineOfCreditFacilityCurrentBorrowingCapacity, especially when a total maximum capacity is also disclosed. The presence of the word 'maximum' signals the intended tag.
[ctx-00018] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: In stock-based compensation sentences, a phrase like 'granted X million of RSUs' indicates ShareBasedCompensationArrangementByShareBasedPaymentAwardEquityInstrumentsOtherThanOptionsGrantsInPeriod for the share/unit count, while 'with weighted average grant date fair values of $Y and $Z' indicates ShareBasedCompensationArrangementByShareBasedPaymentAwardEquityInstrumentsOtherThanOptionsGrantsInPeriodWeightedAverageGrantDateFairValue for each dollar amount.
[ctx-00020] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: In business combination sentences, a phrase like 'consisting of $X million of cash' indicates gross cash consideration and should be tagged with PaymentsToAcquireBusinessesGross, not PaymentsToAcquireBusinessesNetOfCashAcquired. The presence of 'cash' as a component of total consideration without mention of netting indicates the gross tag.
[ctx-00022] helpful=1 harmful=0 :: In stock-based compensation sentences, the verb 'reserved' (e.g., 'reserved a total of X shares for the issuance of equity awards under the Stock Plans') signals CommonStockCapitalSharesReservedForFutureIssuance, not ShareBasedCompensationArrangementByShareBasedPaymentAwardNumberOfSharesAuthorized. The latter is indicated only by explicit 'authorized' language tied to a specific plan.
[ctx-00024] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: In stock repurchase sentences, a phrase like 'repurchased approximately X shares' under a repurchase program indicates TreasuryStockSharesAcquired for the share count, not StockRepurchasedDuringPeriodShares. The presence of a repurchase program context signals treasury stock acquisition treatment.
[ctx-00026] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: In revenue concentration sentences, a percentage describing the portion of revenue derived from a channel, customer, or product (e.g., 'derived 88% of revenue through our channel network') indicates ConcentrationRiskPercentage1 for each such percentage.

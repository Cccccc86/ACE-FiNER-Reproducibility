## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

[err-00002] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Do not confuse principal amounts (dollar values) with stated interest rates (percentages) when tagging debt instruments; use DebtInstrumentFaceAmount for principal and DebtInstrumentInterestRateStatedPercentage for rates.
[err-00003] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Do not tag shares or units issued as consideration in a business acquisition with generic stock-sale tags (e.g., SaleOfStockNumberOfSharesIssuedInTransaction); instead use BusinessAcquisitionEquityInterestsIssuedOrIssuableNumberOfSharesIssued when the issuance funds or is part of an acquisition.
[err-00004] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Do not over-specify revenue as RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax unless the sentence explicitly indicates contract-with-customer scope; when only total revenues are reported (e.g., revenues from acquisitions), use the broader Revenues tag.
[err-00009] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Do not use a single generic repurchase tag for all stock repurchase program figures; distinguish actual repurchases (TreasuryStockValueAcquiredCostMethod), remaining authorization (StockRepurchaseProgramRemainingAuthorizedRepurchaseAmount1), and total authorization (StockRepurchaseProgramAuthorizedAmount1) based on the sentence context.
[err-00016] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Do not tag a rate described as a 'weighted average interest rate' as LineOfCreditFacilityInterestRateAtPeriodEnd or any period-end rate tag; instead use DebtWeightedAverageInterestRate, which explicitly captures the weighted-average qualifier. Period-end tags imply a rate at a specific date, not an average over a period.
[err-00019] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Do not choose PaymentsToAcquireBusinessesNetOfCashAcquired merely because the consideration is described as cash; 'NetOfCashAcquired' means the amount after deducting cash acquired from the acquired entity. When the sentence reports cash consideration paid without mentioning any cash acquired, use PaymentsToAcquireBusinessesGross.
[err-00022] helpful=1 harmful=0 :: Do not tag shares described as 'reserved for issuance' under equity award plans as ShareBasedCompensationArrangementByShareBasedPaymentAwardNumberOfSharesAuthorized. 'Reserved for future issuance' signals CommonStockCapitalSharesReservedForFutureIssuance, whereas the authorized-shares tag applies only when the sentence explicitly refers to shares authorized under a plan.
[err-00023] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Do not tag the number of shares repurchased under a stock repurchase program as StockRepurchasedDuringPeriodShares when TreasuryStockSharesAcquired is available; TreasuryStockSharesAcquired is the more specific tag for shares acquired into treasury under a repurchase program. Reserve StockRepurchasedDuringPeriodShares for broader period repurchase disclosures only when no treasury-specific tag fits.

## CONTEXT CLUES & INDICATORS

[ctx-00001] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: For debt-related entities, dollar amounts representing principal should be tagged as DebtInstrumentFaceAmount, while percentage rates should be tagged as DebtInstrumentInterestRateStatedPercentage.
[ctx-00005] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: When a sentence mentions issuing shares/units to fund or as part of an acquisition, treat the equity issuance as acquisition consideration and select business-combination equity-interests tags rather than generic stock-sale tags.
[ctx-00006] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: When a sentence reports total revenues without specifying contract-with-customer revenue, default to the broad Revenues tag; only narrow to contract-with-customer revenue tags when the context explicitly supports that scope.
[ctx-00007] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: For stock repurchase program disclosures, map the aggregate purchase price of shares actually repurchased to TreasuryStockValueAcquiredCostMethod, the remaining authorized repurchase amount to StockRepurchaseProgramRemainingAuthorizedRepurchaseAmount1, and the total authorized program amount to StockRepurchaseProgramAuthorizedAmount1.
[ctx-00008] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: For dividend disclosures, tag per-share dividend amounts declared as CommonStockDividendsPerShareDeclared rather than aggregate dividend totals or generic dividend tags.
[ctx-00010] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: When a sentence reports an effective income tax rate (e.g., a percentage such as 22.0% or 25.7%) for continuing operations, tag it as EffectiveIncomeTaxRateContinuingOperations rather than a generic tax rate tag.
[ctx-00011] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: When a sentence discloses an estimated range of possible loss in a loss contingency (e.g., 'range of $17,000 to $26,000'), tag the range amounts as LossContingencyEstimateOfPossibleLoss.
[ctx-00012] helpful=2 harmful=0 :: For share-based compensation plan disclosures, distinguish closely related figures by context: total authorized shares under the plan (e.g., '2.9 million shares authorized') map to ShareBasedCompensationArrangementByShareBasedPaymentAwardNumberOfSharesAuthorized; shares available for future grants (e.g., '909,570 shares available') map to ShareBasedCompensationArrangementByShareBasedPaymentAwardNumberOfSharesAvailableForGrant; annual vesting percentage (e.g., '25% per year') maps to ShareBasedCompensationArrangementByShareBasedPaymentAwardAwardVestingRightsPercentage; and vesting period (e.g., 'four years') maps to ShareBasedCompensationArrangementByShareBasedPaymentAwardAwardVestingPeriod1.
[ctx-00013] helpful=7 harmful=0 :: When multiple similar US GAAP tags could apply to a figure, read the surrounding sentence context to determine the exact nature of the entity (e.g., total authorized vs. available for grant, percentage vs. period, principal vs. rate) and select the most specific tag from the provided options rather than a broader generic tag.
[ctx-00014] helpful=1 harmful=0 :: When the same numerical entity (e.g., '107' shares) appears in multiple sentences describing the same underlying event, apply the same US GAAP tag to that entity across all sentences to maintain consistency, even if the surrounding wording differs (e.g., one sentence mentions sale and another mentions proceeds).
[ctx-00015] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: For shares issued during a period, prefer the period-specific tag StockIssuedDuringPeriodSharesNewIssues over transaction-specific tags like SaleOfStockNumberOfSharesIssuedInTransaction, especially when the shares are newly issued and the sentence describes the issuance event rather than a specific transaction structure.
[ctx-00017] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: When a sentence mentions 'weighted average interest rate' for debt instruments (e.g., revolving credit facility, lines of credit), map the percentage to DebtWeightedAverageInterestRate. Match qualifiers like 'weighted average' to tags that explicitly include those terms, and avoid subtype-specific tags (e.g., LineOfCreditFacility...) when a more general but precise tag directly reflects the described concept.
[ctx-00018] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: For share-based compensation disclosures, when a sentence reports equity instruments (e.g., RSUs) granted during a period, tag the number of instruments granted as ShareBasedCompensationArrangementByShareBasedPaymentAwardEquityInstrumentsOtherThanOptionsGrantsInPeriod, and tag the associated weighted average grant date fair value as ShareBasedCompensationArrangementByShareBasedPaymentAwardEquityInstrumentsOtherThanOptionsGrantsInPeriodWeightedAverageGrantDateFairValue. Distinguish these granted-instrument tags from authorized-shares and available-for-grant tags based on the exact wording (e.g., 'granted' vs. 'authorized' vs. 'available').
[ctx-00020] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: For business acquisition cash consideration, distinguish gross vs. net by whether the context mentions cash acquired: if the sentence states cash paid as part of consideration and does not mention cash acquired being deducted, tag it as PaymentsToAcquireBusinessesGross; reserve PaymentsToAcquireBusinessesNetOfCashAcquired for cases where the net cash outflow after deducting cash acquired is explicitly described.
[ctx-00021] helpful=1 harmful=0 :: For share-based compensation disclosures, distinguish 'reserved for issuance' from 'authorized under the plan': when a sentence states shares were 'reserved' for issuance of equity awards (e.g., 'reserved a total of 52,147,732 shares for the issuance of equity awards'), tag it as CommonStockCapitalSharesReservedForFutureIssuance. Reserve ShareBasedCompensationArrangementByShareBasedPaymentAwardNumberOfSharesAuthorized for sentences that explicitly refer to shares 'authorized' under a specific plan (e.g., '2.9 million shares authorized under the 2016 Plan'). The verbs 'reserved' vs. 'authorized' are the decisive signal.
[ctx-00024] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: For stock repurchase program share counts, distinguish the share-count tag from the dollar-value tag: shares actually repurchased/acquired under the program map to TreasuryStockSharesAcquired, while the aggregate cost of those shares maps to TreasuryStockValueAcquiredCostMethod. When a sentence reports a share count (e.g., '1,675,000 shares repurchased'), prefer TreasuryStockSharesAcquired over StockRepurchasedDuringPeriodShares because the context is a repurchase program and the shares are acquired into treasury.
[ctx-00025] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: For authorized share disclosures, distinguish common vs. preferred stock by the explicit class named in the sentence: 'shares of common stock authorized' maps to CommonStockSharesAuthorized, while 'shares of preferred stock authorized' maps to PreferredStockSharesAuthorized. Do not use a generic authorized-shares tag when the share class is specified.
[ctx-00026] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: When a sentence reports a percentage of revenue or business derived from a specific source (e.g., '88% of revenue was derived through the channel network'), tag it as ConcentrationRiskPercentage1 rather than a generic percentage or revenue tag. The presence of a percentage tied to a concentration source (customer, channel, product) signals concentration risk disclosure.

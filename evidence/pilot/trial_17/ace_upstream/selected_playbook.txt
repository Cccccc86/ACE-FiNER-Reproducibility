## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

[err-00003] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Do not tag an ownership percentage acquired by investors in a change-of-control transaction as BusinessAcquisitionPercentageOfVotingInterestsAcquired; if the context is an equity method investment, use EquityMethodInvestmentOwnershipPercentage.
